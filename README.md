## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I am an **Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Swami Vivekananda University**, with a strong interest in academic research, emerging technologies, and intelligent computational systems.

My research interests focus on the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Healthcare Analytics**.

I am particularly interested in developing reliable, interpretable, and computationally efficient machine learning models for healthcare prediction, medical image analysis, and intelligent decision-support systems.

My academic interests also include experimental research, algorithm evaluation, scholarly publication, and mentoring students in emerging computing technologies.

- 🎓 **M.Tech in Software Engineering** – MAKAUT, West Bengal | CGPA: 8.32
- 🎓 **B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering** – IMPS College, Malda | CGPA: 9.31
- 🔬 **Research Focus:** Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- 🏥 **Signature Research:** Chronic Kidney Disease Prediction
- 🌱 **Current Interests:** Explainable AI and Sustainable AI

## 🚀 Featured Research Projects

### 🏥 01. Machine Learning-Based Chronic Kidney Disease Prediction

Research on data-driven approaches for early prediction of Chronic Kidney Disease using clinical and laboratory parameters.

**Research Methods:** Random Forest, SVM, XGBoost, KNN, Logistic Regression, PCA and SMOTE.

### 🧬 02. Deep Learning for Medical Image Analysis

Exploring deep learning and transfer learning architectures for medical image classification and intelligent diagnostic support.

**Research Methods:** CNN, ResNet, VGG16, MobileNetV2 and EfficientNet.

### 🔍 03. Explainable Artificial Intelligence for Healthcare

Investigating interpretable machine learning techniques to improve model transparency and explainability in healthcare applications.

**Research Methods:** SHAP, LIME and Grad-CAM.

### 🌱 04. Sustainable and Green Artificial Intelligence

Evaluating computational efficiency, energy consumption, and carbon emissions associated with machine learning experiments.

**Research Tools:** CodeCarbon, Python and Machine Learning.
